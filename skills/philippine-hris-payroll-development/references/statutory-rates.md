# Statutory Rates (2026)

## SSS (Social Security System) — RA 11199, final tranche Jan 2025 held thru 2026

- **Total Contribution Rate**: 15% of Monthly Salary Credit (MSC)
- **Employee Share**: 5% of MSC
- **Employer Share**: 10% of MSC
- **Employer EC**: P10 (MSC <= P14,500), else P30 — booked on top of employer share
- **WISP / MySSS Pension Booster**: MSC above P20,000 is allocated to WISP (SSS Circ. 2024-006)
- **Minimum MSC**: ₱5,000 (for salary ≤ ₱5,250)
- **Maximum MSC**: ₱35,000 (for salary > ₱34,750)
- **Incremental Steps**: ₱500 per bracket
- **Semimonthly payroll**: split monthly shares in half per cut-off so the month totals once

## PhilHealth

- **Total Premium Rate**: 5% of monthly basic salary
- **Employee Share**: 2.5%
- **Employer Share**: 2.5%
- **Minimum Base Salary**: ₱10,000 (Premium: ₱500)
- **Maximum Base Salary**: ₱100,000 (Premium: ₱5,000)

## Pag-IBIG (HDMF)

- **Salary ≤ ₱1,500**: 1% employee, 2% employer
- **Salary > ₱1,500**: 2% employee, 2% employer
- **Maximum Contribution**: ₱200 each for employee and employer (capped at ₱10,000 base salary)

## Withholding Tax (TRAIN Law, RA 10963 — BIR RR 11-2018 Annex E, unchanged 2024–2026)

Monthly brackets: 0–20,833 exempt; 20,833–33,333 15% over 20,833;
33,333–66,667 P1,875 + 20% over 33,333; 66,667–166,667 P8,541.80 + 25% over 66,667;
166,667–666,667 P33,541.80 + 30% over 166,667; over 666,667 P183,541.80 + 35% over 666,667.
Semimonthly: double the income to find the bracket, then halve the tax.
13th-month + bonuses tax-exempt up to P90,000 combined (PD 851).

## Overtime / Night Differential / Holiday (DOLE)

- Ordinary OT 125%, rest-day 130%, rest-day OT 169%, regular-holiday worked 200% (+100% premium),
  regular-holiday OT 260%, special non-working worked +30%, special OT 169%.
- Night differential +10% for 22:00–06:00 work (Labor Code Art. 86).
- Regular unworked = 100% paid; special unworked = no pay.
